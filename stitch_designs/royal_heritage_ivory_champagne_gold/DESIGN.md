## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a stately, luminous luxury rooted in regal Indian heritage, tailored seamlessly for modern digital interfaces. It merges the architectural precision and artisanal richness of royal palaces with a pristine, airy editorial sensibility. 

The aesthetic marries classical symmetry with modern glassmorphic pearl finishes:
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit courtyards, polished marble, delicate gilded filigree, and calm nobility.
- **Audience:** Discerning clientele, high-net-worth patrons, luxury hospitality guests, and connoisseurs of fine jewelry, bespoke fashion, and heritage experiences.
- **Visual Style:** Refined High-End Editorial with Translucent Pearl Glassmorphism. Structural elements rely on generous whitespace, hairline metallic rims, deep midnight typography, and polished gold accents.

## Colors

The palette revolves around radiant warm neutrals, champagne gold metallics, and deep architectural contrast tones.

- **Primary (`#C59A27`):** Champagne Gold. Used for primary actions, decorative crest lines, active indicators, and high-impact accents. Pair with deep shade `#8C6212` for pressed states or gradient stops.
- **Secondary (`#1B2A4A`):** Royal Midnight Navy. Imparts regal gravitas, grounding interactive controls, badge titles, and key contextual icons.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6212`):** Burnished Gold. Reserved for subtle gilded borders, hover states, and warm typographic embellishments.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) and Alabaster (`#F8F5EE`).
  - Elevated Glass Containers: Semi-translucent pure porcelain white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`).
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - Headings & Primary Copy: Deep Onyx Espresso (`#18181B`).
  - Secondary Copy: Muted Basalt (`#3F3F46`).
  - Decorative Borders & Dividers: Gilded Wire (`#E2D9C8`) and Antique Gold Leaf (`rgba(197, 154, 39, 0.28)`).

## Typography

The typography pairs the regal drama of **Playfair Display** with the clean, contemporary precision of **Manrope**.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Playfair Display. Characterized by high-contrast strokes and traditional calligraphic balance. Always use adequate line heights to let ascenders and descenders breathe.
- **Body & Captions:** Set in Manrope. Offers neutral clarity, high legibility on warm porcelain surfaces, and balances the romantic cadence of the serif headings.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase tracked Manrope (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) evokes bespoke luxury engraving and archival cataloging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial 12-column grid system that emphasizes symmetry, structured hierarchy, and spaciousness.

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width is capped at 1320px for balanced sightlines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional cadence where vertical room between sections (`space-xl` and higher multiples) allows images and serif headings to command quiet grandeur without crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via translucent pearl surfaces and sun-drenched, diffuse ambient illumination rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Glassmorphic Pearl Effect:** Surfaces use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` paired with a backdrop blur of `16px` to `24px` and a hairline perimeter border (`1px solid rgba(226, 217, 200, 0.8)`). This yields a shimmering, frosted alabaster slab.
- **Ambient Sun Shadow:**
  - *Low Elevation (Cards, Tiles):* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(140, 98, 18, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Elevation (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus):* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(140, 98, 18, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`
- **Filigree Rims:** Higher-tier elements carry an inset inner light bevel: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an architectural, tailored softness with `roundedness: 1` (0.25rem base radius).

- **Philosophy:** Avoid overly bulbous or circular forms. Sharp architectural geometry, softened by subtle quarter-rem corners, mirrors cut marble tablets and framed heritage architecture.
- **Scale:**
  - Micro components (tags, inputs, small buttons): `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`).
  - Cards, panels, dialogs: `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
  - Feature display containers: `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Exception:** Chips and status indicators may use pill shapes exclusively when representing dynamic metadata tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Metallic Champagne Gold fill (`#C59A27`), typography in `#FFFFFF` with slight text shadow, border `1px solid #B8860B`. On hover, subtle warm gradient glow (`#8C6212` transition) and lift of 1px.
- **Secondary (Royal Navy):** Deep Navy fill (`#1B2A4A`), text in `#FDFBF7`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 154, 39, 0.4)`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#18181B`, border `1px solid #E2D9C8`. Hover introduces an ivory wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) and champagne border (`#C59A27`).

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Pearl Card:** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`, backdrop filter `blur(18px)`, border `1px solid #E2D9C8`. Padding: `space-lg`.
- **Hero Card:** Pearl card styling reinforced with a dual border treatment: outer border `#E2D9C8`, inner offset hairline in `#D4AF37` at 20% opacity.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2D9C8`, roundedness `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#C59A27` with a subtle champagne glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 154, 39, 0.15)`. Text in `#18181B`. Placeholder in `#71717A`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Badge:** Border `1px solid #E2D9C8`, background `rgba(248, 245, 238, 0.8)`, text in `#8C6212`, `label-sm` uppercase styling.
- **Active Filter Chip:** Fill `#C59A27`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #8C6212`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `18px × 18px`, `rounded-sm` corner. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #E2D9C8`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: solid `#C59A27` fill with crisp white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Button:** Concentric ring design. Unchecked: `#E2D9C8` outline. Checked: outer ring `#C59A27`, inner solid gold dot surrounded by a 2px ivory buffer.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Separators:** Subtle ornamental divider with a centered diamond motif or a gradient hairline fading from `transparent` to `#D4AF37` to `transparent`.
- **List Items:** Gentle hover feedback via an alabaster sheen (`rgba(248, 245, 238, 0.6)`).
